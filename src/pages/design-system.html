Design Tokens

Visual Design System

This layout outlines design metrics and variable values configured in our portfolio templates. All system guidelines are fictional demo configurations.

Border Radius Tokens

  • --radius-sm: 8px — Small buttons & badges
  • --radius-md: 16px — Cards & lists
  • --radius-lg: 24px — Hero cards & dialogue boxes

Spacing & Margins

  • Grid Gap: 1.75rem — Card grid separators
  • Body Padding: 1.25rem — Responsive margins
  • Section Margin: 6rem — Hero paddings

Glassmorphism Filters

  • Blur: blur(12px) — Saturation blur filter
  • Opacity: rgba(17,24,39,0.55) — Surface backing
  • Border: 1px solid rgba(...,0.08) — Highlight line
