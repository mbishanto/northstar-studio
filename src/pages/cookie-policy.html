Cookie Policy

This document details fictional cookie and local storage settings compiled for layout demonstration.

1. What are simulated cookies?

Cookies represent small text data files cached by web browsers. This mock portfolio does not transmit actual user tracking data to external ad engines.

2. How we use local data

We leverage local storage to cache client configuration inputs (specifically, whether the cookie notice bar has been accepted or closed) to prevent displaying it repeatedly on subsequent page views.

3. Modifying Cookie Settings

You can clear your local mock settings at any time by emptying your browser's site cookies and local storage database, returning the banner to its default visual state.
